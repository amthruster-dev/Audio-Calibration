# Audio Calibration

Listens to your speaker through the phone microphone and tells you what to change on your equalizer.

## Download

Open the [latest release](https://github.com/amthruster-dev/Audio-Calibration/releases/latest) and download the file `audio-calibration.apk`.

### Android

1. Download `audio-calibration.apk` and open it.
2. If Android blocks the install, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser, then open the file again.
3. Open Audio Calibration and allow microphone access.

## How to use

1. Connect your phone to the speaker by Bluetooth or wire.
2. Tap **Start listening**. The **Mic check** meter should stay in the green.
3. Tap **Run sweep** (about 12 seconds) in a quiet room, from where you listen.
4. Read **What to change** and set your equalizer to match.

You can choose 3 to 24 bands, type your own frequencies, and pick a target curve.
